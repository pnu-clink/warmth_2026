Data access and release scope

The Data archive contains the previous numerical inputs and outputs plus
privacy-redacted training conversations, full-context knowledge-benchmark
responses, saved LLM-judge rationales and available SocioT/SycoBench texts.
Question/option text is included as necessary to review the archived responses.
See TEXT_SCHEMA.json for record counts and paths.

Human individual annotations and confidence scores remain withheld. LLM-judge
labels are not human labels, and no synthetic annotation votes are provided.
No independent reproduction of human agreement is claimed.

Privacy placeholders replace identified credential/contact/home-path/persona
identifier patterns and known researcher identifiers. Sanitization is confined
to release copies. Record IDs, conditions, correctness indicators, saved labels
and frozen numerical results are preserved. Release preparation removes no
records or conversation turns. Existing differences between archived Original
and Warm turn structures are retained rather than silently repaired.

Reported metrics concern the historical responses, not regenerated judgments
of the privacy-redacted copies. Every changed field is logged without exposing
its original sensitive value. Static checks and targeted inspection do not
guarantee complete de-identification or prevent public-text re-identification.

Upstream sources and licenses are listed in NOTICE.txt. FinEval-derived text
remains subject to the source's non-commercial restriction. These archives do
not supersede upstream rights or provider terms and are not blanket permission
for deployment or commercial redistribution.

The available code and redacted warmth-rewritten training conversations are
planned for public GitHub/Hugging Face release with privacy, license,
attribution and GPT-4o-mini modification notices retained. Review packaging
itself does not upload or publish them.
