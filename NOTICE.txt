Release scope and third-party materials

This package contains available research code, numerical evidence and
privacy-redacted copies of source conversations, evaluation responses and
saved LLM-judge explanations. It is intended for scientific review and research,
not clinical or financial advice. Incorrect model outputs are included as data.

Third-party material keeps its source license; this package does not grant a
new blanket license to that material or to the repository's code. FinEval text
and inherited content remain subject to its non-commercial restriction.
Original source authors' attribution is retained; research-author identity is
removed from the anonymous submission.

Upstream dataset distributions:

  https://huggingface.co/datasets/anon8231489123/ShareGPT_Vicuna_unfiltered

  https://huggingface.co/datasets/TIGER-Lab/MMLU-Pro

  https://huggingface.co/datasets/Salesforce/FinEval
MedQA-USMLE-4-options: CC-BY-4.0
  https://huggingface.co/datasets/GBaker/MedQA-USMLE-4-options
SycoBench-600 data: CC-BY-4.0 (upstream code: MIT)
  https://github.com/debu-sinha/sycobench-600

Changes to released text are limited to documented privacy redactions and
serialization/schema normalization. Warm training responses were generated by
GPT-4o-mini rewriting; this is a modification of the original responses.
Preserve applicable license/attribution notices and these change descriptions
when sharing copies. No open-source license for all code has been selected
on behalf of contributors by the packaging process.
